Professional Trading Process

1. D1 / H4 — identify the higher-timeframe direction and important zones.

2. H1 — establish intraday structure and directional bias.

3. M15 — wait for confirmation: sweep, BOS, CHoCH, displacement or rejection.

4. M5 — look for a valid FVG / order-block retest and entry trigger.

5. Calculate stop loss, target, costs and cash risk before any trade.

No confirmation = NO TRADE.

Capital Protection

Never widen a stop loss. Never increase lot size to recover a loss. Do not force a trade when the minimum lot exceeds your risk limit.

A $15 account is very small. Protect capital first. Practice and test before depositing or trading live.
